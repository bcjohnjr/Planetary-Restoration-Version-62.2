# Model scope and limitations — V62.2

## Evidentiary hierarchy

1. **EXTERNALLY_EXECUTED** — machine-run, machine-gated results from the current external climate workloads, including matched-forcing FaIR/Hector Run12.
2. **LEGACY_MECHANISTIC** — retained quantitative calculations from the model lineage that have not been independently calibrated to the current climate-core standard.
3. **LITERATURE_SCREEN** — numerical or conditional screens anchored to published/official sources.
4. **ENGINEERING_SCENARIO** — mass/energy/design constructs requiring deployment validation.
5. **POLICY_SCENARIO / SERVICE_CAPACITY_SCENARIO** — author-defined sensitivities, not forecasts.
6. **DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY / OPEN_SCIENCE_GATE** — retained to guide the next experiment, not suitable for a headline predictive claim.

## Scope lock

V62.2 is a comprehensive planetary-restoration and human-flourishing framework, not a climate-only model. A future revision may not silently remove a registered module. A module can be deprecated only with an explicit reason, replacement/migration note, and completeness-gate update.

The scope includes climate; sea level/cryosphere; ocean pH; coral reefs; fisheries; seaweed carbon, food, habitat, fuel and Grid Harvesting; plastics; eutrophication; blue carbon; engineered and biological CDR; forestry/soil/wildfire/durable wood; food and hunger; meat-inclusive diet scenarios; water/desalination/reuse; healthcare, vaccines/antibiotics and mRNA cancer-vaccine access pathways; population/carrying capacity; education, housing and justice; robot doctors/lawyers/tutors/eldercare and other robotics; energy/grid/tidal; transport/materials; data-centre heat reuse; Carbonite/Proof of Rent/tree reserves/restoration charity; finance/governance stress cases; Africa vulnerability; Los Jardines; and MRV/audit-chain governance.

## Climate-core limitations after Run12

- Run12 harmonizes future non-CO2 forcing between FaIR and Hector from 2027 onward, substantially improving cross-model attribution relative to earlier runs.
- The experiment does not make FaIR and Hector identical: historical states, carbon-cycle parameters, CO2 forcing equations, and temperature response remain model-specific.
- The canonical CDR schedule is not proven globally optimal.
- The canonical schedule does not return CO2 to 280 ppm by 2300 in the matched-forcing FaIR median or Hector. Earlier 280-ppm dates remain inverse/screening target experiments.
- The observed late-horizon FaIR/Hector response separation is a two-model/one-schedule result. Matched-forcing OSCAR and alternative schedules are required before claiming generality.
- A carbon-reservoir decomposition is required before attributing the long-horizon divergence mechanistically to particular land/ocean/permafrost processes.
- Hector's raw ~1.0966 W/m2 FTOT tracking-offset diagnostic reflects absolute-versus-relative forcing bookkeeping and must not be used as the forcing-match gate. The accepted non-CO2 residual is below 1e-5 W/m2.

## Non-climate limitations retained

- Surface-ocean pH remains a diagnostic screen; publication-grade threshold timing requires temperature-coupled carbonate chemistry.
- Coral recovery dates are conditional biological windows, not a guaranteed Great Barrier Reef restoration calendar.
- Fisheries are stock- and management-specific; cultured fish/seaweed habitat are not wild-stock rebuilding.
- Seaweed engineering, Grid Harvesting and fuel pathways require field-scale validation, lifecycle assessment and ecological safeguards.
- Plastic cleanup is pollution/material recovery and is not automatically CDR.
- Carbonite is a policy/finance hypothesis. Settlement envelopes are not equivalent to disposable restoration revenue.
- Proof of Rent, geotagged tree anchoring and tree-value growth are system-design concepts; tree appreciation is not assumed to beat inflation.
- Robot-doctor and robot-lawyer modules model assisted service capacity, not automatic replacement of licensed professionals.
- Universal healthcare, hunger elimination, housing, education and legal access are resource/policy screens, not guaranteed outcomes.
- Legacy energy, maritime, tidal, materials, wastewater, data-centre and banking values require periodic source refresh before use as current market estimates.

## Accounting protections

The model keeps separate ledgers for CDR, avoided emissions, energy, materials, ecosystem recovery, pollution cleanup and human services. Climate-model validation cannot silently upgrade a non-climate ledger. All removals require additionality, MRV and durability/permanence treatment before being counted as CDR.
